# Bus visual reference research

Research date: 25 October 2026. Google Images was opened in the in-app browser and the visible image grids and enlarged previews were inspected. No third-party game models or skins were downloaded. These are reference-driven procedural interpretations, not measured scans or exact replicas. Images mix years and named variants, so uncertain features are identified below.

## Dam Rajina / Dam Rejina / Dham Rejini / දම් රැජිණි

Searches inspected: `dam rejina`, **`dam rajina bus`** (the user's corrected exact query), `dam rajina bus inside`, `dam rajina bus back side`.

The corrected exact query was opened and visually checked in Google Images in Chrome. It confirms the same fuchsia **S & S GROUP / NB-4110** bus identity used in the initial research, including the burgundy/cream cabin, tubular front bullbar, pink side artwork and raised rear spoiler. It introduces no identity discrepancy. Google also mixes unrelated orange buses and game skins into the results; these were excluded. A newer official-account TikTok preview shows additional circular bumper fog lamps and chrome bullbar rails, further supporting the selected front treatment.

### Exterior observations

- Strong recurring identity: vivid fuchsia/magenta pink classic Leyland body, dark purple lower artwork, white/lilac sweeping bird/feather/floral graphics across both long flanks. Chrome horizontal waist/sill rails, rectangular passenger windows with dark rubber surrounds, and long pink roofline.
- Front: rounded raised pink destination crown; large **S & S GROUP** yellow lettering on a dark inset. Small roof lamps above. Two near-flat rectangular windshield panes divided by a chrome vertical mullion; ornate pale windshield lettering.
- Broad black/chrome horizontally ribbed grille with colored sweeping bird-like artwork. Four circular headlights, arranged as pairs inside two square chrome housings. Upright amber front corner indicators.
- Projecting polished tubular bullbar with rounded lower corners, ornamented pink bumper, small hanging green tassels visible in the front photograph. Large chrome mirrors with multiple stalks.
- Rear: pink full-width raised spoiler with tall wing tips; S & S GROUP rear header. Black rear glazing with large pale/pink Sinhala name. Vertical chrome ladder visible at the left edge of the photographed rear. Wide rectangular red LED tail-light arrays flanking the central yellow number plate; large black lower panel bordered in pale trim, decorated bumper and hanging mudflap fringe.
- Number **4110** appears clearly in rear reference and its caption. Front preview reads NB-4110, but the plate prefix is less clear than the digits.
- A visibly older rear image has a large white dual-fan box under the rear window. Another modern image lacks that box and uses a black panel instead. Do not combine both configurations as though contemporaneous.

### Cabin observations

- Tall burgundy/wine-red seat backs with cream/yellow headrests, cream vertical back inserts and pale piping, arranged around a narrow central aisle. The previews suggest paired seat columns; exact row count is not measurable from the images.
- Red/maroon sidewalls and layered ceiling panels. Nested rectangular/octagonal LED outlines in yellow/white, purple, blue and orange; some imagery shows an orange/gold center panel.
- Large cream-framed speaker cabinets, circular dark woofers, and orange/red trim appear in interior previews. Small gold Buddha figure and golden ornaments sit at the front shrine/dashboard, with pink/purple surrounding trim.
- Interior previews span 2022 and later posts and can represent different modifications. Use shared color/material cues without claiming a surveyed seating plan.

## Nil Kirilli / නිල් කිරිල්ලී / Blue Chick 02

Searches inspected: `nil kirilli`, `nil kirilli bus indoor`.

### Exterior observations

- Bright cyan/light royal blue body, pale blue/white flowing bird/feather graphics and thin gold/yellow accents. Classic boxy split-windshield Leyland outline shared with Dam, but independently dressed front and rear.
- Broad rounded rectangular blue header with cursive pale Sinhala name and thin yellow/gold border. Four white rounded-rectangular auxiliary roof lamps in the strongest enlarged front photograph, plus smaller marker lamps on each side.
- Large central chrome slat grille and smaller side vent grilles. Four circular headlights in two squared pale gold/chrome housings; vertical amber indicators at corners.
- Smooth blue bumper with two dark rectangular slots. The strongest source front photo has no projecting Dam-style bullbar.
- Rear previews show a blue raised spoiler, black lower center plaque and horizontal tail-light banks of three small cells per side. Orientation of ladder should be taken from the specific selected rear image rather than inferred from a mirrored preview.
- A Google result description explicitly identifies `UP ND - 4138`; retain the documented 4138 rather than reading blurred plate pixels. Search results also contain Nil Kirilli 1/2 and other buses, so exact identities must be kept distinct.

### Cabin observations

- Blue/indigo seat upholstery with a mottled or floral pale blue pattern. Chrome/silver rails and luggage-rack edges. Blue patterned reflective ceiling panels, small white downlights, blue/purple/green LED strips.
- Circular speaker rings repeat along upper luggage edges; larger speakers appear on an end bulkhead. The enlarged YouTube preview is dated 7 October 2019, so it is historical visual evidence, not proof of the latest cabin configuration.
- Exclude the warm red/orange Maya Kirilli and Nil Suranganavi cabins mixed into the same Google result grid.

### Direct sources surfaced by Google Images

- [Nil Kirilli / Blue Chick 02 front photograph](https://www.facebook.com/100071522951157/photos/614180290976064/) — enlarged 2048 × 2048 preview shows grille, paired lamps, blue bumper, roof lamps and gold accents.
- [Nil Kirilli cabin video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6J8mED01WyI) — Sadesh Pabasara; enlarged Google preview uploaded 7 October 2019, blue patterned seats and reflective ceiling.

## Accuracy boundaries

The visible reference set covers front, side/front three-quarter, rear, passenger cabin, ceiling, speaker equipment and dashboard/shrine cues. It does not establish exact dimensions, underbody components, axle specifications, seat counts, current modification date, hidden surfaces or exact artwork vectors. Bird/feather artwork is an interpretation. Existing chassis geometry can be shared, while each named bus requires its own palette, lettering, lighting, bumper/bullbar and cabin treatment.
