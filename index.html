About ISSARAHATA YANNA

Sri Lankan bus simulator game

ISSARAHATA YANNA, also known by its full name MADA PELIYA ISSARAHATA YANNA, brings Sri Lankan bus culture to your browser. Your next favorite drive: the best bus simulator game in Sri Lanka.

Drive a decorated Sri Lankan bus along coastal roads and through the highlands. Choose daylight, sunset or night, add your own Sinhala or English bus signs, and stop to pick up passengers. Play solo or invite friends to a private convoy with up to 8 buses. It is free to play, with no download required.
